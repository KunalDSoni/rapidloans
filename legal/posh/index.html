POSH Policy

Prevention of Sexual Harassment at the Workplace

Rapid Loans India Pvt. Ltd. is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and inclusive workplace that is free from sexual harassment. This policy is framed in accordance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and the rules made thereunder.

1. Objective

To prevent and prohibit sexual harassment at the workplace, to provide a mechanism for the redressal of complaints, and to ensure a working environment where every individual is treated with dignity and respect.

2. Scope & Applicability

This policy applies to all employees, trainees, interns, contractors, consultants, and visitors, irrespective of gender, across all offices, branches, and work-related locations of Rapid Loans, including off-site events and digital communication channels.

3. What Constitutes Sexual Harassment

Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome act or behaviour, whether directly or by implication, such as:

  • Physical contact and advances;
  • A demand or request for sexual favours;
  • Sexually coloured remarks;
  • Showing pornography or sexually explicit material;
  • Any other unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.

4. Internal Committee (IC)

The Company has constituted an Internal Committee (IC) to receive and address complaints of sexual harassment, in compliance with the Act. The IC comprises a Presiding Officer, internal members, and an external member with experience in matters relating to sexual harassment.

5. Complaint & Redressal Procedure

An aggrieved person may submit a written complaint to the Internal Committee within the time period prescribed under the Act. The IC will conduct an inquiry in a fair, time-bound, and confidential manner, provide both parties an opportunity to be heard, and recommend appropriate action upon conclusion.

6. Confidentiality

The identity of the complainant, respondent, witnesses, and the details of the proceedings will be kept strictly confidential and will not be disclosed except as permitted under the Act.

7. Protection Against Retaliation

The Company prohibits any form of retaliation against a person who files a complaint, supports a complainant, or participates in an inquiry. Any act of retaliation will be treated as misconduct.

8. Action on False or Malicious Complaints

While genuine complaints are encouraged, complaints found to be deliberately false or malicious may attract disciplinary action against the complainant, as provided under the Act. A complaint that cannot be substantiated does not, by itself, amount to a false complaint.

9. Awareness & Training

The Company conducts regular awareness sessions and training programmes to sensitise employees about their rights and responsibilities under this policy and the Act.

10. Contact

To report a concern or for any questions regarding this POSH Policy, please contact the Internal Committee at info@rapidloans.ai.
